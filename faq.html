FAQ

WHAT IS SET APART?

SET APART is a Christian clothing brand built around faith, Scripture, and purpose. Our goal is to create streetwear that carries a message and gives you something meaningful to represent.

HOW MUCH ARE THE SHIRTS?

Every tee in the current SET APART drop is $30.

WHAT SIZES ARE AVAILABLE?

Our current tees are offered in sizes XS through 3XL. Check the Size Guide before ordering to help choose your size.

HOW DO I CHOOSE A SIZE?

Visit our Size Guide for sizing information. Exact garment measurements should always be checked before placing your order.

WHEN WILL MY ORDER SHIP?

Shipping times and order processing details will be listed on our Shipping & Returns page.

CAN I RETURN OR EXCHANGE MY ORDER?

Return and exchange information will be provided in our Shipping & Returns policy. Please review that policy before ordering.

WHAT INSPIRES THE DESIGNS?

Every SET APART design is inspired by Christian faith and Scripture. The Fear Not Tee is inspired by Isaiah 41:10, while the Armor of God Tee is inspired by Ephesians 6:11.
